Organizational Performance

When work slows down, the cause is rarely one person or one tool. We diagnose how work actually flows, then design processes your team can sustain long after we leave.

Book a consultation
The challenge

Everyone is busy. Results still lag.

Handoffs break down, work gets duplicated and nobody can say exactly where time goes. Quick fixes add steps instead of removing them.

We use Continuous Improvement Process Design methods and problem-solving techniques to evaluate your processes and make recommendations for a reliable long-term payoff.

What we do

What you get

  • Organizational assessment

    OVP’s proprietary assessment tools show where performance is strong and where it stalls.

  • Process maps

    Clear maps of how work moves today — and how it should move.

  • Continuous improvement design

    Kaizen-style process design that builds in small, steady gains.

  • Countermeasure tools

    Simple tools that help teams find root causes and fix them for good.

  • Executive scorecards

    A shared view of the indicators that matter most.

  • Standard templates

    Documented ways of working so good practice survives staff changes.

How it works

Four steps, built around your team

  1. Assess

    Use our assessment tools, interviews and data to see how work really happens.

  2. Map

    Map current processes and pinpoint the delays, rework and gaps.

  3. Redesign

    Design improved processes and countermeasures with the people who do the work.

  4. Sustain

    Set up scorecards and routines so improvements hold over time.
