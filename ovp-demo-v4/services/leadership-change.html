Leadership & Change

Change asks more of leaders and teams than any plan admits. We help people understand their strengths, lead through uncertainty and build a culture that keeps improving.

Book a consultation
The challenge

Change stalls when people are left behind.

New structures, new leaders and new ways of working can leave teams unsure of their role. Engagement drops just when you need it most.

We establish workplace norms that uncover your teams’ talents, maximize the organization’s potential and promote performance improvement — and give members and leaders the tools to sustain growth.

What we do

What you get

  • CliftonStrengths assessments

    Individual and team strengths profiles that give everyone a shared language.

  • Team engagement workshops

    Sessions that set shared principles for how your team works together.

  • Leadership training programs

    Custom modules, workbooks and coaching for new and experienced leaders.

  • Executive & team coaching

    One-on-one and group coaching, including ICF-credentialed coaching.

  • Individual development plans

    Practical plans that turn feedback into next steps for each participant.

  • Program evaluation

    Kirkpatrick-model surveys that measure reaction and learning.

How it works

Four steps, built around your team

  1. Discover strengths

    Profile individual and team strengths and listen to how people experience the change.

  2. Design

    Build the program — workshops, modules, coaching — around your goals and culture.

  3. Develop

    Deliver training and coaching, pairing new leaders with experienced ones where it helps.

  4. Measure

    Evaluate what people learned and how they apply it, and adjust.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a strengths-based approach?

Instead of starting from what is missing, we start from what each person does best — then build roles, teams and development plans around those strengths.

Can programs be delivered virtually?

Yes. Programs can combine virtual workshops, in-person sessions and one-on-one coaching.

Are your coaches credentialed?

Our Chief of Staff & Operations is an International Coaching Federation (ICF) ACC-credentialed coach.
