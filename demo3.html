The Optimal Value Proposition (OVP)

Next-Level Consulting For Sustainable Success

OVP Management Consulting Group Inc. is a strengths-based consulting firm that helps small and midsize firms enhance their team dynamics, craft sustainable operations processes, and prepare them to go after larger contracts.

We work with organizations to create the Optimal Value Proposition (OVP) that defines the relationship between customers, collaborators, and the company, with the goal of achieving an organization's vision.

Dimension 01 · Trust

The Customers

Defining an authentic value proposition that aligns client expectations with organizational execution, building enduring trust across public sector and institutional relationships.

Outcome: Enduring Trust & Value Alignment
Dimension 02 · Talent

The Collaborators

Uncovering latent team talents through certified CliftonStrengths® methodology to establish winning workplace norms, elevate employee engagement, and maximize collective performance.

Outcome: Team Dynamics & Engagement
Dimension 03 · Scale

The Company

Crafting sustainable operations processes through Continuous Improvement Process Design (CIPD) methods to evaluate workflows and prepare organizations to successfully capture larger contracts.

Outcome: Scalability & Larger Contracts
