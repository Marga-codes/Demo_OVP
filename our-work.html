Public Sector · State Agency

Delaware Department of Labor

Challenge:
Starting in 2020, support with emergency communications and marketing as COVID-19 forced reassessment of in-office and remote work, and new leadership needed to communicate changes to staff and the public.
What OVP did:
Needs assessment, centralized communications with OVP as Coordinating Director, weekly strategic communications meetings, communications charter and planning documents, town halls, media oversight, and a six-part television series.
Outcomes:
Documented improvement across four years in digital and social engagement, legacy media interviews, leadership exposure, and the "Did You Know…?" campaign.

Enterprise Training · CalPERS

34 Strong

Challenge:
Design, rollout and delivery of educational training modules for CalPERS — a strengths-based training company delivering for the California Public Employees' Retirement System (1.6M+ members).
What OVP did:
Kaizen (Continuous Improvement) process design based on centralized leadership competencies; workbooks, journals, video, survey products and mock modules; Kirkpatrick Model evaluation (Level 1 & 2).
Outcome:
Course design praised by CalPERS for preparing Section Leaders; client requested an accompanying set of courses for entry-level supervisors.

Non-Profit · Strategic Communications

PASO — West Suburban Action Project

Challenge:
Organizational development and strategic communications for a social justice organization serving the LatinX community in suburban Chicago, amid escalating federal immigration raids.
What OVP did:
Needs assessment, interactive engagement model, individual and team coaching, capacity-building workshops, communications norms and charter, executive scorecards and automated process maps.
Outcome:
Management of reputation and media engagement, multiple news hits and national conversations; improved team collaboration through coaching; engaged community onboarding.

Non-Profit · Government Grant Management

Illinois Action for Children

Challenge:
Help the next generation of leaders develop the skills they need — strengthening internal communications, resiliency and succession planning across program and administrative teams.
What OVP did:
Presentations on Strengths-Finder and personal resilience, CliftonStrengths® team workshops, capital-people-strategy alignment, senior-leadership team facilitation, and support to program and administrative staff.
Outcome:
Building and transferring leadership capacity so the organization's mission can continue for generations — as coached with senior leadership, program staff and administrative teams.

National Coalition · Documentary Media

National Coalition of 100 Black Women — Delaware Chapter

Challenge:
Produce a 5-part documentary series on the epidemic of opioid addiction in the African American community across Delaware, as part of a health initiative.
What OVP did:
As writers and co-producers, spent several months in 2024–2025 interviewing community members and filming resident testimony on the impact of opioids across the state.
Outcome:
Sparked a community conversation and surfaced solutions to help communities protect members, particularly younger people, from succumbing to addiction.
